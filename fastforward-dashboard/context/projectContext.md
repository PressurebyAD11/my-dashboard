# Project Context

## What is this?
An internal operations dashboard for FastForward Logistics.
Built as a POC/prototype to demonstrate value before full build.

## Who is it for?
- Primary: VP of Operations (Sarah Chen)
- Secondary: Regional ops managers who attend leadership meetings
- Tertiary: C-suite who may glance at it in board decks

## Key Behaviors
- Sarah opens this on her laptop 5 min before Monday leadership standup
- She glances at KPIs, checks if on-time rate dipped, scans exceptions
- If something looks off, she filters by region to isolate the problem
- She may screenshare in the meeting

## Constraints
- No in-house dev team (we are the dev team)
- Data is static/mocked for POC phase
- Must feel polished enough to present to leadership
